    FARLIGHT

    A procedurally generated neighbourhood of 110 star systems, every one built to real scale: stars in solar radii, planets in Earth radii, orbits in astronomical units, and light-years between the stars. You start above an Earth-like world. Use the FTL drive to cross a system and the hyperdrive to reach another star.

    In FTL, W and S set the throttle and the drive slows itself near massive bodies, so aim at a planet and hold W. Planets and stars pull on you: thrust away or you fall, and touching down faster than 150 m/s destroys the ship. Thrusters burn hydrogen, the FTL drive burns exotic matter, and everything runs on a battery charged by solar panels on the ship's top. Browsers close the tab on Ctrl+W, so use C to descend while thrusting forward.
